Basics

Getting Started

SeedAim Cloud is a professional cloud-based platform for authorized cryptocurrency wallet recovery and blockchain research. It continuously generates and validates BIP39 recovery phrase combinations on remote infrastructure, then checks the corresponding wallet addresses across supported blockchain networks.

Because the processing happens in the cloud, your phone or computer is used only to control the scanner and review results — it does not perform the heavy scanning workload itself.

Getting started takes only a few steps:

  • Create your SeedAim Cloud account.
  • Select the plan that matches the scan speed you need.
  • Open the Scanner page and start a cloud scan.
  • Monitor processed phrases, discovered addresses and scan activity from your Dashboard.

Once the scan is running, you can leave the page — cloud processing continues independently while your subscription remains active.

No. SeedAim Cloud performs the scanning on remote servers, not on your device. After you start a scan, you can close the browser, lock your phone or turn your computer off and return later to check the latest progress.

This avoids constant CPU load, battery drain and device overheating while allowing the scanner to keep working continuously in the cloud.

Operation

Scanner

The scanner continuously creates BIP39 recovery phrase combinations, derives the corresponding wallet addresses and validates them against supported blockchain data. Processing is handled by SeedAim Cloud infrastructure and synchronized with your account in real time.

Your Dashboard shows the amount of processed data, discovered wallet records and the current state of the active scan.

Yes. The browser is only your control panel. Once a scan has started, the actual workload continues on SeedAim Cloud servers even if the page is closed or your device goes offline.

You can return later from the same or another device and continue monitoring the scan from your account.

When there is no active plan, cloud scanning stops automatically. Your account, previously processed statistics and saved scan records remain available in the platform.

After activating a new plan, you can start cloud processing again without needing to keep your device running in the background.

Cloud scan speed is determined by your active plan:

  • Starter — 30,000 addresses/min
  • Pro — 100,000 addresses/min
  • Ultra — 350,000 addresses/min

A higher plan increases the number of addresses processed every minute, giving you significantly greater search coverage over the same period.

Protection

Security & Privacy

Account access is protected through authenticated sessions and server-side security controls. SeedAim Cloud separates account access from your personal cryptocurrency wallets and does not require you to connect a wallet simply to operate the scanner.

For additional protection, use a unique password and never share your account credentials with anyone.

No. You should never enter the recovery phrase, private key or password of your personal wallet into SeedAim Cloud support forms or send it to another person.

The platform generates recovery phrase combinations as part of its own scanning workflow; it does not need the secret phrase of a wallet you already control in order to run a cloud scan.

The app works as an isolated calculation engine. It doesn’t require any dangerous permissions, doesn't access your contacts, photos, or personal files.

Payments

Billing

Available payment methods are displayed directly on the checkout screen when you activate a SeedAim Cloud plan. The exact cryptocurrency networks and payment options may depend on the current billing configuration.

Activation begins after the payment receives the required blockchain confirmation and is detected by the billing system. Confirmation time depends on the selected cryptocurrency, network conditions and the network used for payment.

Once confirmed, the plan status is updated in your SeedAim Cloud account automatically.

First, verify that the transaction was sent to the exact address and network shown on the payment page and that it has been confirmed on-chain.

Confirmed cryptocurrency transactions are normally irreversible at the blockchain level. SeedAim Cloud cannot cancel or pull back a transaction after it has been broadcast and confirmed.

Before sending payment, carefully verify the selected network, destination address and amount shown on the checkout page.
